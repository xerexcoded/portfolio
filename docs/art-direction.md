# Art direction

Visual thesis: warm ivory, forest green, precise typography, and a living botanical sculpture; optimistic technology with a calm professional voice.

Content plan: identity → context → pinned projects and searchable repository directory → PPLX CLI workflow → engineering capabilities → experience → contact.

Interaction thesis: a staggered hero entrance, restrained section reveals on scroll, and meaningful project hover and disclosure transitions. Motion is disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

## Hero artwork

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool, then converted to WebP without visual editing. The source is `public/images/solarpunk-orbit.png`; the site loads `public/images/solarpunk-orbit.webp` (1536 × 1024, approximately 219 KB).

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium developer portfolio website hero, landscape 3:2. Sophisticated solarpunk botanical-tech sculpture representing the harmony of software engineering and nature. Seamless warm ivory off-white studio floor and backdrop, subtle atmospheric depth, no horizon line. A large sculptural toroidal ring of brushed pale aluminum and polished chrome, interwoven with lush realistic fern fronds and velvety green moss. Precise slim architectural orbital rings pass around it, with one small clear glass sphere integrated into the structure. Physically plausible, grounded, meticulously engineered. Premium photorealistic editorial 3D render, architectural magazine aesthetic. Object occupies the right two-thirds, centered at roughly 68 percent across the canvas, with generous empty ivory negative space on the left for website copy. Sculpture fully in frame, breathing room above and at right, soft natural contact shadow. Eye-level camera, moderate three-quarter angle. Natural soft studio daylight from upper left; calm, optimistic, quietly futuristic; elegant reflections, no dramatic glow. Warm ivory, deep forest green foliage, pale brushed silver, subtle muted lime-green highlights. Realistic fern fronds, fine moss texture, micro-brushed aluminum, restrained chrome reflections, optically clear glass. No text, letters, logos, watermarks, UI, or people. Do not depict a website. No neon, cyberpunk, busy background, or extra decorative objects. Botanical detail and precision engineering are the focus.
